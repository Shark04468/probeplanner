## Claim boundary

Supported claims are limited to the locked cohort results, strict constraint
auditing, fail-closed behavior, and minimum probe count within each regenerated
strictly filtered candidate pool.

This release does not support continuous-space global optimality,
subject-specific landmark TRE, bregma/lambda or skull localization accuracy,
individual-animal native-space surgical safety, vascular or ventricular safety
where masks are unavailable, or histological implantation accuracy.
